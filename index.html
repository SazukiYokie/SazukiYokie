📖 Reading Framework

True / False / Not Given

TRUE: The statement matches the passage exactly (usually with synonyms).

FALSE: The statement contradicts the passage. The passage says the opposite.

NOT GIVEN: The information is not in the passage. Don't use outside knowledge!

Skimming vs. Scanning

Skimming is rapid reading for the general overview (Main Ideas). Scanning is hunting for specific facts (dates, names, numbers) to answer a specific question.

📝 Writing Architecture

Task 2: Band 9.0 Body Paragraph

1. Topic Sentence: State your main point clearly.

2. Explanation: Explain *why* or *how* this point is true.

3. Example: Give a specific, real-world example.

4. Result/Link: Link it back to the essay question.

Task 1: The Overview

The most important part of Task 1 Academic is the Overview paragraph. Summarize the main trends, differences, or stages without using specific numbers.

🗣️ Speaking Strategies

The 3 Parts of the Test

Part 1 (4-5 mins): Familiar topics (hometown, studies, hobbies).

Part 2 (3-4 mins): The Cue Card. You get 1 minute to prep, and must speak for up to 2 minutes.

Part 3 (4-5 mins): Abstract discussion linked to Part 2.

🎧 Listening Tactics

Beware of Distractors

Speakers in the recording will often say one answer, and then correct themselves. (e.g., "Let's meet at 5 PM. Oh wait, I have a meeting, let's do 6 PM.") Always wait for the final confirmation!
