IELTS Guide: how the real test works

Practise exactly like the exam day. Timings and question counts below follow the official IELTS format (Academic).

PaperTimeContent
Listening30 min + 10 min to transfer answers4 sections, 40 questions. Audio is played once.
Reading60 min3 passages, 40 questions. No extra time to transfer answers.
Writing60 minTask 1: 150+ words (about 20 min). Task 2: 250+ words (about 40 min). Task 2 counts double.
Speaking11 to 14 minPart 1 interview (4-5 min), Part 2 cue card (1 min prep + 1-2 min talk), Part 3 discussion (4-5 min).

Conversion tables differ slightly between test versions, so treat these as estimates. Your overall band is the average of the four skills, rounded to the nearest 0.5.

Writing is marked on 4 criteria

  • Task Achievement / Response: answer every part of the question
  • Coherence & Cohesion: clear paragraphs and linking
  • Lexical Resource: range and accuracy of vocabulary
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: variety of structures, few errors

Speaking is marked on 4 criteria

  • Fluency & Coherence: speak at length without long pauses
  • Lexical Resource: flexible, precise vocabulary
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy
  • Pronunciation: clear, natural stress and intonation

How to use this site

  1. Start with a single section / passage to learn the question types, then move to a full test under timed conditions.
  2. In Writing and Speaking, press Generate to get an exam-style task, then ask the AI examiner to grade it. Read the feedback and rewrite.
  3. Check My Scores & Tests weekly: aim for steady progress toward your target band.
